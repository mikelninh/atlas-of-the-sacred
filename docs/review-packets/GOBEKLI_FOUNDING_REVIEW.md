# Founding review packet 002

## Göbekli Tepe — gathering, habitation and unresolved worlds

**Version under review:** claim graph at `agent/gobekli-evidence-map`  
**Expected effort:** 60–90 minutes  
**Primary discipline:** Early Neolithic archaeology of Upper Mesopotamia  
**Secondary disciplines:** architecture, archaeozoology, public archaeology, conservation and Turkish heritage practice

## Purpose

This packet does not ask whether the portal is impressive. It asks where its claims, categories and interface ideas outrun the archaeological record.

## Review questions

### 1. Chronology and terminology

- Are the tenth- and ninth-millennium phases described accurately enough for public use?
- Does “monumental communal or special-purpose building” reflect current terminology?
- Where are round, oval and rectangular categories too simplified?
- Should “apparent domestic” be strengthened, weakened or replaced?

### 2. Site and regional context

- Does the portal sufficiently place Göbekli Tepe inside the wider Taş Tepeler and Upper Mesopotamian landscape?
- Does any language still reproduce the isolated “zero point of history” narrative?
- Which Turkish publications or project sources are essential additions?

### 3. Pillars and imagery

- Is “anthropomorphic” correctly bounded by observable arms, hands, belts and loincloths?
- Which species labels are secure enough for an interface?
- Where could the Stone / Body / Relation interaction imply more than placement and depiction support?
- Which competing interpretations deserve representation?

### 4. Work and provisioning

- Are quarry and pillar-scale statements accurately sourced?
- Does the cereal-processing claim distinguish observation from feasting hypotheses?
- Does the portal overstate how much labour organisation can be reconstructed?

### 5. Building biographies

- Is the mixed account of intentional deposition, later construction, collapse and sediment movement defensible?
- Which contexts support deliberate placement most clearly?
- Which language should replace the popular claim that the entire site was deliberately buried at once?

### 6. Social and ritual interpretation

- Does the portal keep cooperation, leadership, hierarchy, ritual and dwelling appropriately open?
- Is “ritual” being used too broadly or too timidly?
- Where do contemporary Western categories distort the material record?

### 7. Conservation and stewardship

- Does the portal represent the 2025 UNESCO conservation decision fairly?
- Is excavation restraint framed as responsible archaeology rather than obstruction?
- Whose living, local and institutional authority should be visible in the final experience?

## Prototype review

The first prototype is a schematic **Circle of Presences** with three modes:

1. **Stone** — architectural arrangement and scale;
2. **Body** — observable anthropomorphic features;
3. **Relation** — animal imagery and competing readings.

Please identify:

- features that are too specific for a schematic reconstruction;
- missing caveats;
- visual choices that imply roofs, ceremonies, identities or sequences not established by evidence;
- better ways to communicate uncertainty without removing wonder.

## Required response record

Each point should be recorded as one of:

- accepted;
- accepted with modified wording;
- further evidence required;
- unresolved disagreement;
- declined with rationale.

Participation does not imply endorsement. Public attribution, quotation and affiliation require explicit consent and apply only to the reviewed version and scope.
