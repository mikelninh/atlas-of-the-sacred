# Founding review packet 001 — Giza

## Review purpose

Challenge the accuracy, classification and public interpretation of the Giza portal before it is described as independently reviewed.

This packet does not ask whether the experience is impressive. It asks where the experience is inaccurate, misleading, insufficiently qualified or culturally shallow.

## Suggested effort

60–90 minutes for one disciplinary section, plus optional written follow-up.

Scope, timing, attribution and compensation—where applicable—must be agreed before review begins.

## Portal under review

- Giza expedition route: `/sites/giza/`
- Editorial registry: `/editorial/`
- Relevant project documentation: `docs/GIZA_V2.md`

## Section A — Egyptology and archaeology

### Claims

- `giza-khufu-tomb`
- `giza-scale-materials`
- `giza-royal-complex`
- `giza-complex-route`
- `giza-subsidiary-pyramids`
- `giza-boat-pits`
- `giza-known-interior`

### Questions

1. Is each statement accurate at its current level of precision?
2. Does the terminology reflect current Egyptological practice?
3. Are modern labels such as “King’s Chamber,” “Queen’s Chamber” and “queen’s pyramids” sufficiently qualified?
4. Does the portal over-centre the Great Pyramid at the expense of the wider royal, settlement and funerary landscape?
5. Which important primary or authoritative sources are missing?

## Section B — Construction and engineering

### Claims

- `giza-tura-transport`
- `giza-worker-city`
- `giza-construction-method-open`
- `giza-scale-materials`
- `giza-big-void`
- `giza-north-face-corridor`

### Questions

1. Does the phrase “construction as a living system” clarify evidence or conceal missing mechanics?
2. Could the conceptual route families be mistaken for reconstructed historical methods?
3. Is the wording about ramps, hauling, levering and combined systems appropriately cautious?
4. Does the interface distinguish a detected low-density space from a structurally understood chamber?
5. What construction or load-management alternatives should be represented as competing claims?

## Section C — Landscape and surveying

### Claims

- `giza-ahramat-branch`
- `giza-cardinal-alignment`
- `giza-alignment-method`

### Questions

1. Is `probable` the right status for the reconstructed Ahramat branch claim?
2. Does the portal fairly separate reconstructed ancient landscape from direct observation?
3. Is the cardinal-alignment statement sufficiently sourced and bounded?
4. Does the stellar surveying model receive too much visual authority relative to competing models?
5. Which alternative surveying explanations should be added before public comparison?

## Section D — Public interpretation and heritage

### Questions

1. Where does the portal privilege modern mystery over Egyptian historical context?
2. Are Egyptian institutions and scholarship visible enough in the source hierarchy?
3. Could any phrase reinforce common colonial, pseudoarchaeological or exoticising narratives?
4. Does the final question about what “we” build respectfully return to the present, or appropriate the monument into a generic modern lesson?
5. What local educational or heritage voices should shape the next version?

## Required review response

For each material concern, provide:

- affected claim, module or phrase;
- severity: factual error, misleading inference, missing qualification, missing source, terminology, cultural framing or accessibility;
- recommended change;
- supporting source or rationale where available;
- decision: approve, approve with notes, request changes or reject.

## Conflict statement

Please disclose relevant:

- authorship;
- excavation or institutional involvement;
- employment or funding;
- commercial relationships;
- advocacy positions;
- close relationships with the project team.

## What publication means

A completed review may be recorded publicly only under the agreed attribution terms.

The Atlas will publish:

- the bounded scope;
- the decision;
- requested changes;
- project responses;
- unresolved disagreements;
- the version reviewed.

It will not imply that the reviewer endorses unrelated claims, future versions or the platform as a whole.
