## Göbekli Tepe milestone

RC2 includes the first implementation of the second deep portal:

- a five-chapter evidence-backed roadmap;
- 13 Göbekli Tepe claims across chronology, regional context, habitation, pillars, animals, quarrying, provisioning, building histories, social organisation, meaning and conservation;
- the Circle of Presences with Stone, Body and Relation modes;
- evidence drawers attached to interactive features;
- explicit warnings that the schematic is not an archaeological reconstruction;
- a bounded specialist review packet.

The portal does **not** present “first temple,” “religion created civilisation,” one site-wide ceremonial burial, fixed pillar identities or astronomical-catastrophe readings as established facts.
