# Atlas of the Sacred V1.0 release plan

**Target launch:** 22 September 2026  
**Closed beta:** 8 September 2026  
**Release candidate:** 15 September 2026

## Release promise

A visitor can enter through a human need, complete **The Common Thread**, go deep at **Giza**, inspect the evidence behind what they encounter, and leave seeing humanity’s relationship to meaning differently.

V1.0 is not the complete atlas of civilisation. It is the first complete proof that our method works.

## The V1.0 experience

1. **The Living Centre** — enter through Remember, Cooperate, Heal or Awaken.
2. **The Common Thread** — a 10–15 minute guided journey through seven human movements.
3. **Giza** — the first deep portal combining engineering, landscape, cosmology, evidence and open mystery.
4. **The Evidence Lens** — every factual claim exposes its status, sources, review date, interpretation and limits.

## Definition of done

- GitHub and Vercel contain and deploy the same product version;
- the four launch routes work on mobile and desktop;
- 100% of published factual claims expose sources, review dates and limits;
- no critical accessibility, security or factual-integrity failures remain;
- beta thresholds are met or a waiver is documented publicly;
- rollback is tested;
- the first post-launch update is prepared.

## Product success thresholds

- at least 70% of beta visitors complete The Common Thread after starting;
- at least 60% can accurately explain the central idea afterward;
- at least 50% open an evidence panel;
- at least 40% say they would return for another site or journey;
- no unresolved expert objection remains against an “established” flagship claim.

## Explicit cut list

The following do **not** block V1.0:

- dozens of deep portals;
- VR or photorealistic 3D worlds;
- accounts, profiles or social feeds;
- paid membership;
- a native mobile app;
- a complete planetary knowledge graph;
- automated AI publication without human review.

## Governing principle

> Build what helps humanity remember, cooperate, heal and become more conscious—and build it beautifully enough that people want to enter.

The release date moves before the integrity standard does.
