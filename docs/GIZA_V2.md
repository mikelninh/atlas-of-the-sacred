# Giza V2 — Flagship portal

## Product promise

Create the most compelling evidence-led public journey through the Great Pyramid: beautiful enough to invite wonder, disciplined enough to keep every mystery honest, and structured enough to deepen over time.

## Visitor arc

1. **Arrive at scale** — understand the monument as a landscape-scale human project.
2. **Follow the system** — quarry, river logistics, settlement, administration and unresolved raising sequence.
3. **Recover the river world** — move between the Old Kingdom floodplain and the present landscape.
4. **Walk the royal complex** — harbor edge, causeway, temples, subsidiary monuments and pyramid.
5. **Orient to the sky** — distinguish measured cardinal alignment from debated surveying methods.
6. **Enter the pyramid** — move through known spaces and scientifically detected voids without inventing their purpose.
7. **Return to the present** — ask what we are building now that deserves to outlive us.

## Evidence contract

- Reuse the published Giza claim registry as the factual source of truth.
- Distinguish established, probable, contested and open-mystery material in every module.
- Label interface models and spatial diagrams as schematic or interpretive.
- Never turn absence of evidence into evidence for a dramatic explanation.
- Keep sources and explicit limits one action away from each load-bearing statement.

## V2 scope

- expedition overview and progress rail;
- richer construction systems lab;
- landscape phase slider;
- guided royal-complex procession;
- conceptual alignment error simulator;
- expanded interior navigator with known/detected/unresolved layers;
- final reflection and shareable thesis.

## Not in this sprint

- photorealistic 3D;
- numerical workforce or construction-time claims not represented in the registry;
- definitive ramp reconstruction;
- speculative chamber contents;
- new deep-site portals.
