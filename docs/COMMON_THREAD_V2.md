# The Common Thread V2

## Product promise

Create a guided experience that helps visitors recognise recurring human questions across sacred architecture while protecting the historical and cultural difference of every place.

The journey must never imply one lost civilisation, one secret religion or one universal explanation.

## Visitor arc

1. **We Gather · Göbekli Tepe** — coordinated attention and communal monumentality.
2. **We Watch · Stonehenge** — shared time, horizon and recurring return.
3. **We Invite Light Back · Newgrange** — midwinter illumination entering enclosed space.
4. **We Build for Eternity · Giza** — material, social, spatial and royal systems aligned toward endurance.
5. **We Descend · Ħal Saflieni** — subterranean depth, mortality and altered attention.
6. **We Shape a Living World · Angkor** — water, settlement, cultivation, power and sacred architecture in relationship.
7. **We Walk the Teaching · Borobudur** — movement through a specifically Buddhist monumental pedagogy.
8. **We Return · Us** — choose Remember, Cooperate, Heal or Awaken as a practical doorway back into life.

## V2 interaction contract

Each threshold contains:

- a culturally specific photograph and location;
- one load-bearing, evidence-labelled claim;
- an explicit cultural-boundary statement;
- a unique symbolic gesture;
- a visible warning that the gesture is not an archaeological reconstruction;
- optional access to full claim cards and sources;
- a reflection and a transition into the next threshold.

## Seven symbolic gestures

- **Gather:** relate four positions to a shared centre.
- **Watch:** move light along a horizon and mark its return.
- **Invite light:** guide a beam toward an enclosed chamber.
- **Endure:** coordinate stone, water, labour and orientation.
- **Descend:** pass through three symbolic depths.
- **Flow:** connect water, settlement, cultivation and temple.
- **Walk:** advance through three symbolic terraces.

These gestures are interfaces for attention. They are not simulations of historical ceremonies, surveying, hydraulics or pilgrimage.

## Editorial safeguards

- Difference appears before cross-cultural comparison.
- Every threshold names what should not be inferred.
- Established evidence is visually distinct from interpretation.
- The user may skip every gesture; completion is not treated as spiritual attainment.
- No culture is reduced to a generic mindfulness metaphor.
- Site-specific expert review remains necessary before the journey is called institution-grade.

## Accessibility and interaction

- Thresholds can be selected directly from the seven-step constellation.
- Left and right arrow keys move between thresholds when focus is not inside a control.
- Interactive controls use practical touch targets and visible focus states.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove entrance animations and transitions.
- The active threshold is represented in the URL hash for orientation and sharing.

## Review gate

Before public institutional release:

1. test every gesture with keyboard, touch and reduced motion;
2. test comprehension with first-time visitors;
3. have each cultural-boundary statement reviewed by an appropriate specialist or authoritative heritage voice;
4. test whether visitors can explain both a recurring human question and at least one important cultural difference;
5. log corrections and disagreements transparently.
