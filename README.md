# Atlas of the Sacred

**An immersive, evidence-led exploration of sacred architecture and humanity’s search for meaning.**

> We neither dismiss mystery nor manufacture it.

## Explore the public field edition

### https://mikelninh.github.io/atlas-of-the-sacred/

The current experience includes:

- the common human story connecting sacred sites without flattening their differences;
- an interactive cross-cultural connection engine;
- a deep Giza construction journey;
- a changing-Nile landscape visualisation;
- an interactive explanation of the axis mundi;
- a guided journey through the Great Pyramid’s interior;

## Product direction

Atlas of the Sacred is growing into a trusted, immersive atlas of temples, pyramids, ritual landscapes and the recurring ways human beings have shaped stone, light, sound, water and movement into meaning.

The long-term product combines:

- cinematic exploration;
- structured archaeological claims;
- traceable primary and institutional sources;
- expert review and revision history;
- interactive maps, timelines, comparisons and reconstructions;
- public access to foundational knowledge, with ethical paid depth around it.

## Repository status

The public field edition is deployed from [`site/index.html`](site/index.html) through GitHub Pages. The larger Next.js production foundation—with reusable site, claim, source, journey and editorial components—is preserved separately while its reviewed modules are progressively brought into this repository.

## Editorial principle

Every factual claim should expose:

1. its evidence status;
2. traceable sources;
3. its latest review date;
4. interpretation separated from observation;
5. what the evidence does **not** prove.

Different places. Different peoples. One enduring quest to align earth, heaven and the human heart.
